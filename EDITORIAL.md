# Editorial Policy

Shores Dashboard is an independent civic information project. These rules are non-negotiable and version-controlled so they can't drift over time.

## Core rules

1. **Headlines + 1–2 sentence blurb + link.** Never republish full articles. Blurbs paraphrase; they do not quote at length.
2. **Always link to the source.** Every item on the site has a visible "Read at [source]" link.
3. **Neutral tone.** Describe what happened. Do not editorialize about whether a Council vote, ordinance, or news event was good or bad.
4. **No partisan framing.** No party labels added where the source did not include them. No characterizations of officials or candidates as "progressive," "conservative," "establishment," etc.
5. **No candidate ads. No political ads. Ever.** Sponsorships are local businesses and civic organizations only.
6. **No display ads.** The site does not run programmatic or banner advertising.
7. **Footer disclaimer must always read:** *"Independent civic project — not affiliated with the City of St. Clair Shores."*

## Council meeting recaps

Recaps of Council meetings:

- Lead with what was decided (votes, ordinances passed/tabled, public hearings opened).
- Note the vote tally if available (e.g., "passed 6–1, with [Member] dissenting") — factual record, not framing.
- Summarize public comment by topic and rough volume (e.g., "Twelve residents spoke, mostly opposed to the rezoning"), not by individual speaker unless a public official.
- Link to the meeting video and agenda packet.
- Do not characterize the *quality* of debate or the motivations of officials.

## Corrections

If something on the site is wrong, fix it and add a `corrected:` timestamp to the data file. Do not silently edit.

## What we do NOT cover

- Crime blotter (covered well by Patch and C&G; we link to theirs)
- Sports
- Restaurant reviews / lifestyle
- Anything outside St. Clair Shores city limits, except where the City is directly involved (e.g., county-level decisions affecting SCS)

## Sponsor vetting

Every sponsor is reviewed before going live. Sponsors that conflict with the editorial policy (political committees, candidates, businesses with active legal action against the City) are declined.
